About

Who I am

I'm Harfateh, an Engineering I Co-op student at McMaster University. I grew up playing competitive sports and finished high school at Sheldon-Williams Collegiate in Regina, Saskatchewan, before moving to Hamilton for engineering.

For almost two years I worked operations at Tuxedo Courts, a busy multi-sport facility, where I ran the front desk, set up and changed over courts, and kept leagues and tournaments on schedule. That job, plus being an athlete, taught me to solve problems fast and communicate clearly. This past summer I interned at UPS International in Punjab, India, building, wiring and testing electrical control panels.

Engineering I is a common first year, so I choose my discipline at the end of it. This term I've modelled parts in Autodesk Inventor, designed a gripper for a robot arm, written games in Python and pitched a product with my team. I'm most drawn to structural and system problems.
